# Auqat-e-Namaz — notes for testers

Thank you for trying this. It is a deliberately small prayer-times app: times, next prayer, Qibla, adhan — nothing
else. It works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads, and no network access at all.

## Installing

1. Open the `Auqat-e-Namaz-x.y.apk` file on your phone (from the message, email or download).
2. Android will say the app is from an unknown source. Allow installs from that app (a one-time setting), then Install.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. (The warning appears because the app is
   not on the Play Store yet; it contains no network code at all.)
4. Open the app. Allow **location** ("While using the app", *Precise*), **notifications**, and on the follow-up
   screen choose **Allow all the time** so times can update while you travel.
5. Go outside or near a window for the first GPS fix (up to a minute).

Recommended once: Settings → **Exclude from battery optimisation…** (Samsung, Xiaomi and others otherwise delay alarms).

## What to check

* Do the times match your mosque's timetable? Set the same calculation method and Asr rule in Settings.
  Small differences of a minute are normal; anything more, please tell me the city, date, prayer and both values.
* Does the adhan sound at prayer time, with the phone locked and untouched? Does volume-down stop it?
* Is the Qibla arrow right when you stand somewhere with a known direction?
* Does the Islamic date change at Maghrib (not at midnight)?
* When you travel, do the times follow you after you open the app?

## Sending feedback

Say what phone you have (model, Android version — shown in Settings → About phone), what you expected, and what
happened. The app version is at the bottom of its Settings screen. Screenshots help.
