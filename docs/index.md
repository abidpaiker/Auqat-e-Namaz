# Auqat-e-Namaz

**Prayer times · next prayer · Qibla · adhan — offline, free, no ads, no accounts, no tracking.**

A deliberately small Android app for people who pray wherever they happen to be. It calculates the times on the
phone from your position, so it works with no signal at all, and it follows you as you travel.
