# Auqat-e-Namaz — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

Auqat-e-Namaz is a free, open-source Android app that shows Islamic prayer times, the next prayer, the Qibla
direction and plays the adhan. It is written and published by Abid Paiker.

## The short version

The app collects no data. It has no internet access, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you do
in the app ever leaves your phone.

## What the app uses, and why

**Location.** Prayer times and the Qibla direction depend on where you are. The app reads your position from the
phone's location service (GPS) and keeps only the most recent position, on the phone, to calculate the times. If you
allow location "all the time", the app can also take a fresh position shortly before a prayer while it is not on
screen, so the times stay correct when you travel. You may instead enter a location by hand in Settings and deny
location permission entirely. Your position is never sent anywhere; the app has no network permission, so it cannot.

**Notifications and alarms.** Used only to alert you at prayer times, as you choose in Settings.

**Audio files you choose.** If you pick your own adhan recording, the app copies that one file into its private
storage so it can play it later. It reads no other files.

**Device sensors.** The compass (magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope) is read only while the Qibla screen is
open, to turn the arrow.

## What the app does not do

It does not connect to the internet. It does not collect, store or share personal information. It does not use
cookies, identifiers, advertising or analytics libraries, or any third-party code. It does not track you. There is
no server: there is nothing for anyone to request, sell or breach.

## Data on your phone

Your settings and last position are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Uninstalling
the app deletes all of it.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Open source

The complete source code is public at https://github.com/abidpaiker/auqat-e-namaz under the MIT licence, so anyone
can verify every statement above.
